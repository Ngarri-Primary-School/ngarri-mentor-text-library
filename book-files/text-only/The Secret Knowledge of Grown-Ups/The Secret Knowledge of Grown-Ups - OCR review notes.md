# The Secret Knowledge of Grown-Ups — OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `The Secret Knowledge of Grown-Ups - David Wisniewski.pdf`
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- The PDF contains 52 pages; the story (field logs plus eight grown-up rules) runs across PDF pages 8–50.
- Story text was extracted from the embedded text layer and joined across line breaks.
- Punctuation, quotation marks, apostrophes and ellipses were retained as embedded; likely scan-OCR substitutions in this Internet Archive digitisation were kept, not silently corrected (e.g. "atonic cows" for atomic, "louja" for Iowa, "Prance" for France, "teek" for seek, "clanger" for danger, "leaue/arriues", "GROIDN-UP/GROUJN-UP RULE").
- The mattherd ballad and the finger-gang/CHO lists were kept in printed order.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the continuous narrative plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Cover/title text (pages 1–2), the coded transmission pages (3–7, mostly illegible symbol strings), blank and illustration-only pages, and the closing page 50 (garbled fragments only, apparently a sign-off line) were excluded from the story transcript.

## Confidence

Narrative text: medium-high confidence — the layer is substantially readable but carries Internet Archive scan-OCR noise, so proper nouns and rule numbers should be confirmed visually (rule numbers on the log pages are partly garbled: #3, #37, #56, #62 read with low confidence). Pixel-level visual comparison against the rendered scans is still pending: the image-reading tool was unavailable during this pass, so a visual pass should be done before treating this transcript as final. Illustration summaries are orientation notes only; consult the original PDF for exact visual evidence.
