# Night Tree - OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `Night Tree (Eve Bunting).pdf`  
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- The complete scanned source was reviewed from beginning to end.
- Every section containing narrative text was visually checked against the rendered scan.
- Several passages in the embedded extraction were substantially scrambled or incomplete. Those passages were restored from the visible source images.
- OCR errors involving joined words, missing words, misplaced lines, quotation marks, apostrophes, capitalisation and punctuation were corrected against the scan.
- Names, song titles and distinctive printed phrases were retained as shown, including `Luke's Forest`, `Oh Come, all ye Faithful`, `Old MacDonald Had a Farm`, `E-I-O` and `tree smell`.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the complete continuous story prose and a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Cover text, handwritten inscriptions, Internet Archive notices, publisher details, jacket material, decorative marks and illustration-only pages were excluded from the story transcript.

The searchable text-only PDF was generated from this checked transcript; it is not a replacement for the illustrated source when composition, colour, character expression or visual detail matters.

## Confidence

Narrative text: high confidence after visual comparison. Visual summaries: concise orientation notes only; consult the original PDF for exact illustration evidence.
