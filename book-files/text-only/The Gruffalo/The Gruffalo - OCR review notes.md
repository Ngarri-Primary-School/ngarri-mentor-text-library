# The Gruffalo — OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `The Gruffalo - Julia Donaldson.pdf`
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- All 26 PDF pages rendered at 150 dpi; every story page (3–25) read visually, word for word.
- The PDF has no text layer (pure image scan), so the transcript rests entirely on the visual read.
- The source is a FlipHTML5 flipbook capture of a Lydia Johnston upload; edition quirks ("his tongue is black", truncated "gruffal . . .") were kept as printed.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the complete story verse with printed line breaks. The cover (page 1), two illustration-only spreads (pages 2 and 6) and back-cover publication matter (page 26) were excluded.

## Confidence

Narrative text: high confidence after full visual comparison. Consult the original PDF for colour, composition and illustration detail.
