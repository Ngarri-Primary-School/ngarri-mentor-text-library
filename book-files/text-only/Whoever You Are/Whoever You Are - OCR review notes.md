# Whoever You Are — OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `Whoever you are (Mem Fox).pdf`
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- The PDF contains 36 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 11–33.
- Story text was extracted from the embedded text layer and kept line-by-line as printed; decorative border and display-type glyphs on every story page were excluded as non-text noise.
- Short verse lines are spread one opening per idea, so line breaks follow the layer’s line layout rather than being joined into prose.
- The layer carries stray fragments (“So—” on page 11, trailing asterisks marking repeated refrains); repeated refrain asterisks were not transcribed as text.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story verse plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Copyright and cataloguing data (page 6), the dedication (page 7), digitisation matter (page 8) and the review quotation (page 36) were excluded from the story transcript.

## Confidence

Narrative text: medium-high confidence — the verse lines are legible and consistent with the published text, but every story page mixes decorative glyphs into the layer, so a visual pass should confirm line breaks and wording. Pixel-level visual comparison against the rendered scans is still pending: the image-reading tool is unavailable during this batch, so a visual pass should be done before treating this transcript as final.
