## Story text

There are little ones just like you
all over the world.

Their skin may be
different from yours,
and their homes may be
different from yours,

and their lands may be
different from yours.

Their lives may be
different from yours,

and their words may be
very different from yours.

But inside,
their hearts are
just like yours,

whoever they are,
wherever they are,
all over the world.

And they laugh just like you.

You may be different,

and they may be different,
wherever you are,
wherever they are,
in this big, wide world.

Pain is the same,
and blood is the same.

Smiles are the same,
and hearts are just the same,
wherever they are,
wherever you are,
wherever we are.

## Meaningful visual evidence

- The opening spreads introduce children — little ones just like you — all over the world, in homes, lands and lives that differ from the reader’s.
- The middle spreads contrast outward differences (skin, homes, lands, lives, words) with the inward sameness of hearts and laughter.
- The closing spreads gather the theme — pain, blood, smiles and hearts the same wherever they are, wherever you are, wherever we are — in this big, wide world.
