## What was checked

- The PDF contains 32 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 6–27, with the about page on 28 and an illustrated index on pages 29–30.

- The layer carries Internet Archive scan-OCR noise, worst in the small-type side panels: page 7 (emu height/weight figures partly garbled), page 9 (single-word illustration label), page 13 (symbol strings), page 15 (hatchling-size panel garbled), page 17 (diet panel garbled), page 19 (speed panel garbled), page 21 (feather-change panel garbled), page 22 (zigzag panel tail illegible), page 25 (danger panel garbled), page 27 (independence panel garbled).

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story's continuous prose plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Copyright/imprint matter (page 4), the title page (5), and the illustrated index (pages 29–30) were excluded from the story transcript; the about page (28) is included as a closing factual panel since it is continuous selectable text.

## Confidence

Narrative (large-type) text: high confidence — clean and internally consistent. Side-panel figures and small-type facts: low-medium confidence — garbled figures were bracketed, not guessed. Pixel-level visual comparison against the rendered scans is still pending: the image-reading tool was unavailable during this pass, so a visual pass should be done before treating this transcript as final.
