# Ralph Tells a Story — OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `Ralph Tells a Story -- by Abigail Hanlon .pdf`
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- The PDF contains 40 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 10–36.
- Story text was extracted from the embedded (scanned-OCR) text layer and joined across line breaks.
- Typeset narration was retained as embedded, including minor substitutions (“|” for I, “Pll” for I'll).
- Handwritten illustration text was checked page by page and flagged where the layer is garbled rather than silently reconstructed.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story's narration plus legible fragments of the handwritten stories, with a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Cover and publishing matter (pages 1–2, 5), copyright and cataloguing data (page 6), the dedication (page 7), the archive digitisation stamp (page 8), library stamps, the author biography (page 39) and closing matter (pages 38, 40) were excluded from the story transcript. The back-cover summary (page 2) is quoted once at the head of the transcript for orientation.

## Confidence

Narrative text: medium confidence — the typeset narration is clean, but the handwritten pages (10, 12–13, 26, 29–33, 37) are heavily garbled in the text layer and only fragments could be recovered honestly; these passages MUST be verified visually against the PDF before classroom use. Pixel-level visual comparison against the rendered scans is still pending: the image-reading tool is currently unavailable, so a visual pass should be done before treating this transcript as final. Illustration summaries are orientation notes only; consult the original PDF for exact visual evidence.
