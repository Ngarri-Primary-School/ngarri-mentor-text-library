# The Alphabet Tree — OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `The_alphabet_tree_Lionni,_Leo,_1910_z_library_sk,_1lib_sk,.pdf`
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- The PDF contains 39 scanned pages; the story runs across PDF pages 11–36.
- Every page containing narrative text was visually checked against the scan.
- Page 25 and the ending on page 36 were missing from the connector’s first continuous OCR result and were restored from the page images.
- OCR punctuation and spacing errors were corrected, including quotation marks, apostrophes, em dashes and joined words.
- The wording and capitalisation of `PEACE ON EARTH AND GOODWILL TOWARD ALL MEN` were retained exactly as printed. Curriculum guidance about discussing the historical phrase remains in the approved teaching content.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story’s continuous prose. It also records meaningful word-building examples embedded in the illustrations. Front cover text, jacket copy, library markings, copyright information, isolated alphabet letters and scan artefacts were excluded from the story transcript.

## Confidence

Narrative text: high confidence after visual comparison. Illustration-word lists: representative rather than exhaustive because some words run beyond page edges or are deliberately scattered. Use the original PDF whenever exact placement, colour or visual composition matters.
