## What was checked

- The PDF contains 40 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 8–34.
- Story text was extracted from the embedded (born-digital) text layer and joined across line breaks.
- Punctuation, quotation marks, apostrophes and the "TIL-LIE, TIL-LIE, TIL-LIE!" chant capitals were retained as embedded.
- No normalizations were needed — the story layer is clean throughout. Minor illustration-layer fragments on story-adjacent pages (23, 27, 35) carry no story prose and were excluded.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story's continuous prose plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Cover and title matter (pages 1, 5, 7), the Internet Archive digitization credit (page 2), the author biography (page 36), library stamps (page 39) and the back-cover blurb (page 40) were excluded from the story transcript. Blank and illustration-only pages carry no text and contribute no prose.
