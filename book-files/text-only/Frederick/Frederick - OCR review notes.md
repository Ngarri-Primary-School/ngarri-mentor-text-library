## What was checked

- The PDF contains 28 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 5–26.
- Story text was extracted from the embedded (born-digital) text layer and joined across line breaks.
- Punctuation, curly quotation marks, apostrophes, em dashes and ellipses were retained as embedded.
- The seasons poem (PDF page 24) was kept line-by-line as printed.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story's continuous prose plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Copyright and cataloguing data (page 3), the navigation page (page 4), the author biography (page 27) and the praise page (page 28) were excluded from the story transcript.
