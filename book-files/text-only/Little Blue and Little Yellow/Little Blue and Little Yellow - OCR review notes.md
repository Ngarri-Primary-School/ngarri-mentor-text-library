# Little Blue and Little Yellow — OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `Little_blue_and_little_yellow - Leo Lionni.pdf`
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- The PDF contains 48 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 7–44.
- Story text was extracted from the embedded (born-digital) text layer and joined across line breaks.
- Punctuation, quotation marks, apostrophes, em dashes and ellipses were retained as embedded.
- Normalizations applied: page 10 layer reads "best friend 1s little yellow" → transcribed "is"; library rubber-stamp fragments (WALLA WALLA / RURAL LIBRARY DISTRICT variants on pages 7, 9, 15, 35, 36, 42, 44) and stray scan glyphs (pages 3, 19–21, 35–36) were excluded as noise, with the underlying story words ("He looked here / and there / and everywhere") joined into prose.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story's continuous prose plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. The back-cover blurb (page 2), a garbled pre-title scan page (page 3), title and dedication matter (page 5), copyright data (page 6), the parent-teacher note (page 45), the author biography (page 47) and ISBN matter (page 48) were excluded from the story transcript.

## Confidence

Narrative text: high confidence — the embedded text layer is clean and internally consistent once stamp noise is removed. Pixel-level visual comparison against the rendered scans is still pending: the image-reading tool returned an authentication error during this pilot, so a visual pass should be done before treating this transcript as final. Illustration summaries are orientation notes only; consult the original PDF for exact visual evidence.
