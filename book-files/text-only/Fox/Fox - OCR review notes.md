## What was checked

- The PDF contains 39 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 10–36.
- The scan has no embedded text layer (fully-scanned copy), so every story page was read visually from renders at 150 dpi.
- All 39 pages were inspected: 21 story-text pages transcribed, 6 illustration-only story spreads noted, and all front/back matter identified for exclusion.
- Tesseract OCR was run over all pages as a rough location map only; the hand-lettered text OCR'd poorly and was not used for wording — final wording is from visual reads.

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the verified story prose plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Cover (page 1), front illustrations (pages 2–3, 5–6), title page (page 7), copyright/imprint data (page 8), dedication (page 9), praise/blurb matter (page 4), author biographies (page 37), and closing endpaper illustrations (pages 38–39) were excluded from the story transcript.

## Confidence

Narrative text: high confidence — every story page was read directly from the rendered scan, including the vertical/rotated text blocks on pages 13 and 24. Residual risk sits in hand-lettered ambiguities (e.g. em-dash vs hyphen forms, capitalisation of display words), so a second pair of eyes on pages 10, 13 and 24 before classroom use would be prudent. Illustration summaries are orientation notes only; consult the original PDF for exact visual evidence.
