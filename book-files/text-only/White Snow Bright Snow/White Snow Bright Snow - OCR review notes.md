# White Snow Bright Snow — OCR and visual review notes

**Source:** `White Snow, Bright Snow (Alvin Tresselt).pdf`
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## What was checked

- The PDF contains 38 pages; the story runs across PDF pages 5–33, with the closing verse repeated on PDF page 36.
- Story text was extracted from the embedded text layer and joined across line breaks; the “OceanofPDF.com” watermarks on each page were excluded.
- The opening and closing verses (pages 5 and 36) were kept line-by-line as printed.
- Suspected text-layer errors were kept as embedded and normalised only for capitalisation and punctuation: “it come” (p. 9), “five, eight, fen” read as “five, eight, ten” (p. 9), “Slipped” capitalisation (p. 17), “Feet…Them…Plaster…Wouldn’t” capitals (p. 18), “bright stars felled the night” read as “filled” (p. 20), “the burn” read as “the barn” (p. 17), “moil” read as “mail” (p. 30).

## Scope decisions

The searchable transcript contains the story’s continuous prose and verses plus a short index of meaningful visual evidence. Edition and copyright matter (page 2), the title page (page 3), the contents table (page 4) and the publisher review request (page 37) were excluded from the story transcript.

## Confidence

Narrative text: high confidence — the embedded text layer is clean and internally consistent apart from the suspected character errors listed above.
