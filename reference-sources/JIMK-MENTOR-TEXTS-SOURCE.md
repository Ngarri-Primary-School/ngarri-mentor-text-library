## Purpose

This private repository contains transcriptions, extracts, mentor-text lists and professional-reading notes that may help locate language features or check a passage while preparing Ngarri Mentor Text Library content. Useful title-specific files include *Night Tree*, *Owl Moon*, *Fireflies*, *Smoky Night*, *The Very Hungry Caterpillar*, *The Tenth Good Thing About Barney*, *Wilfrid Gordon McDonald Partridge* and many others represented in the Ngarri catalogue.

## Use conditions

- Use the preserved transcriptions in the locations and formats approved by the school.
- Treat a transcription as a checking aid, not proof that the complete published book or its illustrations were inspected.
- Before writing book-specific content, verify the complete school-held book through the restricted Google Drive and visually inspect any illustration used as evidence.
- Check transcription wording against the verified school copy. Record discrepancies rather than silently combining editions.
- Store only brief lawful quotations, book-specific analysis, source provenance and approved teaching records in the Ngarri project.

## Scope note

The folder mixes title transcriptions, short extracts, poetry, mentor lists and larger professional-reading compilations. Its presence does not establish that every file is complete, visually checked or suitable for a particular Ngarri curriculum connection. Apply the intake and quality checks in `docs/CONTENT-AUTHORING-GUIDE.md` and `.codex/skills/ngarri-mentor-text-enrichment/SKILL.md` for each book.
