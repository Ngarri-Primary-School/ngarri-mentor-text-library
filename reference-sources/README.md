## Read-only source audit

The user identified three legacy project locations: the iCloud Mentor Texts project, the former Obsidian vault and the Department of Education OneDrive Mentor Texts project. A read-only SHA-256 inventory found:

| Location | Files | Size |
|---|---:|---:|

| Former Obsidian vault | 23 | 0.6 MB |
| OneDrive Mentor Texts project | 341 | 161.8 MB |
| **Total copies** | **832** | **332.4 MB** |

There are 485 unique file hashes and 265 duplicate-hash groups. Every duplicate group crosses project locations, confirming that much of the iCloud and OneDrive material is mirrored rather than separate evidence. The source folders were not edited.

The professional-reading and school-PDF set contains 22 copies representing 10 unique documents. Matching copies in iCloud and OneDrive have identical hashes. The structured reference set is also duplicated; nine files are byte-for-byte identical and `available-texts.json` differs only in the historical Mac base path recorded for each copy.

## Material deliberately excluded

The following remain outside GitHub, except for the explicitly approved text-only continuity archive under `../restricted-reference/`:

- full picture-book PDFs, scans and page images;
- *The Power of Inquiry* by Kath Murdoch;
- *The Reading Strategies Book*;
- *Strategies That Work*;
- *The Writing Thief*;
- *6 + 1 Traits of Writing: The Complete Guide*;
- the raw Oz Lit recommendation PDF; and
- the Year 5 planner files, which contain live school links and identifiable student filenames.

The project may retain derived, limited structured records from an excluded source when they are needed for legitimate school planning and clearly identify their provenance. Do not treat derived selection indicators, AI suggestions or framework crosswalks as verbatim source wording.
