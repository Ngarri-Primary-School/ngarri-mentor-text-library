## Teaching ideas — newly drafted routines, pending approval

The routines below are original drafts built around the selected learning intentions. They are not copied publisher activities. Timing is a planning estimate. The reviewer chooses and records the actual book passage/spread before either routine becomes ready to teach.

### T1. Change two words, explain the effect

**Purpose:** Word Choice (W2). **Time:** 20 minutes. **Grouping:** teacher model, then pairs. **Starting level:** Entry, with support.

1. Revisit a teacher-selected passage. Ask students to describe its effect on the reader.
2. Model replacing two words with plausible alternatives. Think aloud about why the effect changes.
3. Pairs try another replacement and explain their choices.
4. Individually, students revise one sentence of their own writing for a chosen effect.

**Notice learning:** Can the student explain what a word does, rather than simply calling it a better word?

**Before approval:** Record the passage and confirm that W2 is approved. This is a reusable teaching routine; its Fox-specific application depends on that passage selection.

### T2. Evidence, interpretation, alternative

**Purpose:** Inferring (R1). **Time:** 20 minutes. **Grouping:** pairs, then discussion. **Starting level:** Discussion.

1. Display a teacher-selected spread containing clues to a character's intention.
2. Students record an observable clue separately from what they think it suggests.
3. Pairs offer a second possible interpretation and decide what additional evidence would help.
4. Students write a brief interpretation supported by two clues.

**Notice learning:** Can the student distinguish observation from inference and revise an interpretation when the evidence changes?

**Before approval:** Record the spread and confirm R1. Do not mark disagreement with the teacher as wrong when a student can support a different reading.
