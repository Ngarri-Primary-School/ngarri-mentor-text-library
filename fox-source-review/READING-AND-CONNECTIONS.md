## Corrections established by the text

The character is Magpie, not Kookaburra. Magpie speaks the reciprocal promise about eyes and wings. Fox has multiple speaking turns. The text names haunted eyes, not scars. The seasonal compression and three approaches are present. The ending describes starting home, not an observed reunion or apology. The supplied typography and absent illustrations cannot support visual-analysis claims.
